openCert Vocabulary

Namespace: https://opencert.eu/ns#

Classes

  • oc:ArchitectureBuildingBlock – A capability required by the AI Act.
  • oc:SolutionBuildingBlock – A reusable asset implementing one or more ABBs.
  • oc:ReusabilityLevel – Classification of reuse level.
  • oc:ReusabilityOrgType – Reusable by any organization of the same type.
  • oc:ReusabilityOrganization – Reusable only by the publishing organization.

Properties

  • oc:implementsABB – relates an SBB to the ABB it implements.
  • oc:hasMaturityLevel – maturity level 1‑5 (ISO 33020).
  • oc:hasAccreditationLevel – accreditation level 1‑5.
  • oc:accreditedBy – authority that granted the accreditation.
  • oc:coversMeasure – AESIA guiding measure covered.
  • oc:hasSectorPurpose – links an ABB to a sector purpose (from DPV).
  • oc:reusabilityLevel – indicates the reuse level of an SBB.
  • oc:targetOrgType – target organization type for reuse.
  • oc:adaptationRequired – effort needed to adapt (none, minimal, moderate, major).
